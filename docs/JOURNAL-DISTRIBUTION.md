The defining question of this age is not only who gets access to powerful AI. Access without ownership is still rent.

That is why I am building CurXor — a desk appliance where your personal algorithm lives on metal you own. Desk crew. Approve gates. Local by default. Pre-revenue. Early. Real operators starting to sit down.
